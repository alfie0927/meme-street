Costs

Your broker's commission is in cTrader's symbol information or your account's terms. It is charged when a trade opens and again when it closes; Ballast counts both. cTrader's currency-conversion fee is counted automatically.

Check before sending

The loss shown is what hitting the stop costs, commission included. A stop is a price level, not a guaranteed price: in a fast market or over a gap the fill can be worse.

How the size is worked out

Ballast finds the largest size whose loss at your stop, commission included, is no more than the risk you set. It always rounds down to a size the symbol allows, takes every exchange rate on the side that makes the loss larger, and values the loss at the rate it would have when the stop is hit. If even the smallest allowed size would lose more than you set, it says so and places nothing. If your loss limits leave less than you asked for, the size is made smaller and the review says so.

Pip: the usual small step of a currency price (0.0001 on most pairs, 0.01 on yen pairs). Point: one unit of an index or crypto price. Lot: the symbol's standard size (100,000 units of a currency pair). R: the money you risk on one trade; a target of 2 makes twice that if it is reached.

Exact figures from your firm (optional)

These come from your firm's dashboard. Today's figures are forgotten when the day resets. Where a figure is given, it is used exactly.

How the limits work

Firms that fund traders close an account that loses more than a set amount in a day or in total, and they count open trades too. Enter your firm's numbers here. Ballast shows how much is used and how much is left, makes every new trade small enough that hitting its stop keeps you inside both limits (with open trades counted at their stops), and stops placing orders when a limit is reached.

Firms differ: some count the day from the opening balance, some from the higher of balance and equity; some reset at midnight in Prague, others at the broker's server midnight. Read your firm's rules and match them here. A stop that fills worse than its price (in a fast market or over a gap) can still take you past a limit. Ballast blocks its own orders only: trades placed elsewhere in cTrader are counted, not blocked.

What R means

Each trade placed from Ballast carries the money it risked. A trade that made twice its risk is +2 R; one stopped out is about -1 R. The average R over many trades is your average result per trade: above zero, the way you trade has made money so far; below zero, it has not, whatever the win rate. A position closed in parts counts as one trade.

About the sessions

The usual hours of the four main forex sessions, each in its own city's time so daylight saving is right everywhere. Prices usually move most when London and New York overlap. Your broker's hours for a symbol can differ (cTrader's symbol information shows them); index and crypto markets keep their own hours.

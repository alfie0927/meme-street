What if you could borrow a memory?

MemoryBank lends memories like library books, from people who chose to share them. It earns lending fees, charges for memory vaults, and sells skill memories recorded from experts.

People use it to learn faster and train without real-world risk. Its edge: the largest memory vault. The risks: privacy rules, fake memories, and rival DreamWeave.

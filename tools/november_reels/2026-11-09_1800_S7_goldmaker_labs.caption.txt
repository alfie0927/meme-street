This company turns lead into gold. For real.

GoldMaker Labs runs industrial alchemy: lead in, gold out. It sells gold bars, supplies ultra-pure gold to chipmakers, and licenses 'made gold' jewellery.

Its edge is a secret process nobody has cracked. The risks: make too much and the gold price falls, energy costs are huge, and fellow alchemists RuneWorks have recipes of their own.

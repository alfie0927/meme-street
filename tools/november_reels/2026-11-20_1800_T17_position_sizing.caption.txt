How much should you put in one trade? Here's the simple maths.

Say you have 1,000 MB and risk 1% per trade: 10 MB. If your stop is 5% away, your position is 200 MB, because 5% of 200 is 10.

That way, even ten losses in a row leave you with about 90% of your account. No single trade can sink you.

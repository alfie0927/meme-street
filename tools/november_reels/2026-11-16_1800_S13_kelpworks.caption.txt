This farm is under the sea.

KelpWorks grows vast kelp farms on the sea floor and sells kelp as food, fertiliser and bioplastic packaging. That's food and materials grown without any farmland.

Its edge: the biggest sea-floor farms and the know-how to run them. The risks: storms and currents, Abyssal Habitats wanting the same sea floor, and kelp prices.

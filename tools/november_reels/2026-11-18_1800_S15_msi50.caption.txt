One number that tells you how the whole market's doing.

The MSI 50 is Meme Street's main index, tracking a basket of fifty companies in a single price you can trade like one stock.

Because it's spread across many companies, one bad day for one stock matters less. What moves it: broad sell-offs, the biggest companies, and economic data.

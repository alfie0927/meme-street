When these bands squeeze tight, pay attention.

Bollinger Bands are a 20-period average with a band two standard deviations either side. When the market goes quiet, they squeeze, and a squeeze often comes before a bigger move. It just won't tell you which way.

And touching the upper band isn't a sell signal on its own. Strong trends can ride it for a long time.

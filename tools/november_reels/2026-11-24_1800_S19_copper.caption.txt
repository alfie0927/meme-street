Why do traders call it Doctor Copper?

Copper is one of five commodities on Meme Street. In the real world it's in almost every electric cable, plus buildings and electric cars. Because it's used everywhere, its price is seen as a health check for the economy.

What can push it down: a slowing economy, new mines and weak building data.

This company steers hurricanes.

StormShield's coastal towers push storms back out to sea. It earns from yearly city protection fees, deals with insurers, and emergency call-outs when a storm forms.

That means less damage and safer coastlines. Its edge: a tower network that takes years to build. The risks: a storm it can't steer, rival CloudSeed, and neighbours who don't want storms pushed their way.

Your stop keeps getting hit by normal noise?

ATR, the average true range, measures how far price typically moves in one candle. Low when the market's calm, high when it's wild. It says nothing about direction, only size.

Many traders use it to size stops, for example two ATRs from the entry, so ordinary noise doesn't knock them out.

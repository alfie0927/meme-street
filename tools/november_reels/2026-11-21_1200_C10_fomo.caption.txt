Everyone's talking about it and the price is flying. Do you jump in?

That's fear of missing out. By the time everyone's talking about a move, much of it has often already happened, and you can end up buying near the top.

Have a plan for missing a move: wait for a pullback, or find the next setup. There's always another trade.

Most new traders put their stop in the wrong place. Or skip it.

Too tight, and ordinary noise stops you out of a good trade. Too wide, or none at all, and one bad trade can wipe out weeks of gains.

A common rule: just beyond the level that proves your idea wrong, like below the last swing low. Meme Street has chart tests on exactly this.

A sharp rally, then a quiet pause. Here's what it might mean.

A bull flag starts with a sharp rally, the pole, then a quiet drift lower on light volume, the flag. When price breaks out of the flag, the trend often continues.

Flags fail too: watch out if it drifts too deep or the pullback has heavy volume. A stop below the flag limits the damage.

Two candles that can warn you a move is running out of steam.

The hammer: a long lower wick and a small body near the top. After a fall, buyers fought back. The shooting star is the opposite: after a rise, sellers pushed back.

Location matters. At support or resistance, they're worth noticing. And wait for the next candle to confirm.

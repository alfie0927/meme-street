```text
MACD looks complicated. It's really just two moving averages talking to each other.

It's the gap between a fast and a slow average. The signal line smooths it out: crossing above means momentum is turning up, below means it's turning down. Shrinking histogram bars mean momentum is fading.

Like any average, it lags, and it chops in sideways markets.
```

```text
When these bands squeeze tight, pay attention.
```

```text
Your stop keeps getting hit by normal noise?

ATR, the average true range, measures how far price typically moves in one candle. Low when the market's calm, high when it's wild. It says nothing about direction, only size.

Many traders use it to size stops, for example two ATRs from the entry, so ordinary noise doesn't knock them out.
```

Why does price keep stopping at the same spot?

Support is where buyers have stepped in before. Resistance is where sellers have. The more a level is tested, the more traders watch it.

When resistance breaks, it often becomes support. But levels are zones, not exact lines, so a stop goes beyond the level, not right on it.

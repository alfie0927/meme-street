Why do so many traders care about 61.8%?

A Fibonacci retracement is drawn from a swing low to a swing high, marking 23.6, 38.2, 50 and 61.8% of the move. When price pulls back, traders watch those levels for a pause.

They're not magic. They work best alongside other support, and price misses them all the time.

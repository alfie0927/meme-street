You can be wrong most of the time and still not lose money. Here's how.

Compare what you risk with what you aim to make. Risking 1 to make 3 is very different from risking 1 to make 1. At 1 to 3, you can be wrong three times out of four and still roughly break even.

But the target has to be realistic, like the next resistance level.

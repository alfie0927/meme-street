This company's cranes have wings.

Ember Dragon Ranches breeds and trains dragons for heavy lifting. It rents them to builders by the hour, trains the handlers who fly them, and sells dragon fire to steelworks as forge heat.

Its edge is decades of careful breeding. The risks: dragons eat a lot, insurance isn't cheap, and Griffin Air Freight competes for the same jobs.

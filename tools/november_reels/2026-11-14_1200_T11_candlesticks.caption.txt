Every chart is made of these. Here's how to read one.

Each candle shows four prices: the open, the close, the high and the low. The body runs from open to close, and the wicks show the extremes. Green closed higher, red closed lower.

Together, they tell a story. A long upper wick means buyers pushed up, and sellers pushed it back.

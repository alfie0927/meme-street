Stop trading every hammer. Check where it formed first.

The hammer: a long lower wick and a small body near the top. After a fall, buyers fought back.
The shooting star is the opposite: after a rise, sellers pushed back.

At support or resistance, they're worth noticing. And wait for the next candle to confirm.

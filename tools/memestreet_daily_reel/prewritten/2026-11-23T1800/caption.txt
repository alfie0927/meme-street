A sharp rally, then a quiet pause. Here's what it might mean.
A bull flag starts with a sharp rally, the pole, then a quiet drift lower on light volume, the flag.
When the price breaks out of the flag, the trend often continues.
Flags fail too: beware a drift too deep or heavy volume on the pullback. A stop below the flag limits the damage.

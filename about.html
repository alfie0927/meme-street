How it works

News moves prices

Rumours, earnings, product launches, lawsuits, industry-wide shocks and rare big catalysts, written about companies with their own CEOs, products and rivals. Read the wire, then decide.

Trade anything

Buy or short stocks, the MSI 50 index (the 50 biggest companies), sector indices, ETFs, 2x and 3x products and wild little "moonshots". Limit, stop-loss and take-profit orders work for you.

Fair by design

No trade, ours included, moves a price. News has no hidden direction: the expected move of every story is zero. We test this by simulating hundreds of markets with dozens of trading strategies and checking that none of them wins.

Compete

A fresh leaderboard every day, a daily contest everyone enters with the same paper money, quests and achievements. Only the ranks and badges are for show; they never touch your balance.

How the house earns

The house is on the other side of every trade and charges 0.1% on each trade. Nothing else is built in: no hidden spread and no price steering. Buying and holding costs you almost nothing; trading all day costs you the fee each time.

Questions

Is it real money?

Not yet. Meme Street is in closed testing and memebucks (MB) are play money while we test. This page will change before that does.

Are these real companies?

No. Every company, person, product and headline is invented. The numbers behave like a market, but nothing here is investment advice.

Can I lose everything?

You can lose your balance, especially by shorting or trading moonshots. Shorting needs the amount held as collateral, and a short that loses too much is closed automatically.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The game adapts to phone screens, and you can watch the live market without signing in.

# Daily-game directories and listings: texts to send

For the growth plan, Part 4 (week 3 for the daily-game directories, week 4 for the listings). You send them;
free listings only, never a paid "featured" spot. Before sending, check each site's submission page and rules on
the day; they change.

## The texts

**Name:** Daily Chart by Meme Street

**Tagline** (under 60 characters):
```
Like Wordle, for trading: one chart a day
```

**Short description** (under 160 characters):
```
One mystery stock chart a day, the same for everyone. Buy, sell or short for two minutes as the candles print, then see where your return ranks.
```

**Long description:**
```
Every day there is one new chart, and everyone gets the same one. You start with 1,000 MB of practice money and have two minutes: the candles print one a second, and you buy, sell or short as they come. When the time is up you see your return, your place, and how everyone else did, with a streak to keep and a result to share. The chart comes from a made-up stock, so nobody can look it up. A new one starts at midnight UTC. Free to play, no sign-up needed, and practice money only: MB have no cash value.
```

**Tags:** daily game, trading, stock market, charts, finance, puzzle, education, wordle-like

**Category:** puzzle or finance (whichever the site has); not "betting" or "crypto".

**Screenshots** (phone size): the chart mid-game, the result card with the green and red squares, the share
picture ("Can you beat it?"). I can make these in the browser when you are ready to send.

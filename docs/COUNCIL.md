# Council verdicts

Shared memory for the idea council. Run `/council <idea>` in Claude Code; the Judge appends an entry below.

## 2026-10-03 - Meme Street real-money pivot
- **Idea:** Trading game with invented companies (150+, 30 industries), live LLM news wire, long/short, limit/stop orders, 2x/3x leveraged products. House is counterparty and takes 0.1% fee per side; daily contest drives retention. Play-money now, real-money business is the goal.
- **Verdict:** FIX FIRST
- **Biggest risk:** Players cannot verify the operator isn't rigging news and prices, so nobody deposits real money, and play-money testing can't reveal that. Legal (gambling/derivatives licensing) is the second gate.
- **10-minute test:** Post a $5 refundable "founding member" paid-contest link (Stripe payment link) to the existing testers and see who clicks and pays; no code needed.
- **Flip to BUILD:** Run the paid-entry test with a daily signed hash of the news/price seed published (commit-reveal) and get a quick legal opinion on the cheapest structure (skill contest, closed currency). BUILD if >=5% of testers with 3+ active days pay $5-20 within 7 days and ~40% repeat, and legal cost is under ~$150k / 6 months. Below 1-2% it is a free toy; do not build real-money rails.

## 2026-10-13 - Direction change (not a council verdict)
- The real-money plan above was dropped. Meme Street becomes a site for learning to trade: 1,000 MB to start, MB packages (100 to 10,000 MB) sold and never cashed out, challenges that award MB, and a market rebuilt for realism (drift, regimes, macro data, technical structure). See `ROADMAP.md`. The verdict above is kept as history; its risks (rigging, gambling licences) mostly fall away without cash-out, and new ones (selling currency to people who lose, minors, consumer law) take their place. A fresh `/council` run on the new plan would be worthwhile.

## 2026-11-17 - Meme Street as a learn-to-trade game earning from Pro and Expert
- **Idea:** A browser game for learning to trade on a fictional stock market that never closes (157 invented companies, a path of lessons, a two-minute Daily Chart that needs no account), earning only from Pro (US$9.99 a month) and Expert (US$29.99 a month), found through automated Instagram reels. The owner wants a real income, has 2 to 4 hours a week, and belongs to no trading, teaching or gaming community.
- **The facts it was judged on:** 15 human accounts after six weeks, one of them the owner's; 1 new in 17 days; 4 others ever traded; one person came back to the Daily on 12 days; no pass ever sold (revenue US$1.99, the owner's own test); reels reached 12,192 strangers in 29 days for 2 link taps; every send by hand still unsent.
- **Believer:** sell a class or society licence instead ("a whole trading day in one lesson": a 30-minute game day fits a period, which real-market simulators cannot do); the free Daily is the demo; about 100 classes at Stock-Trak's price is about US$60,000 a year.
- **Skeptic:** kill Pro and Expert (free real-ticker simulators exist, learners ask for real tickers, the paid tiers sell leverage and bots on generated candles); do not build the class version either, because the free projector lesson already delivers the pitch, teachers' alternatives are free, candlestick trading is on no syllabus and every paying example is American. The pattern to break: six weeks of building while a 20-minute message to friends stayed unsent, and every "customer" so far an AI panel.
- **Investor:** would not invest today: no stranger has paid and none has been asked. All routes together, under 10% odds of US$3,000 a month within 24 months; the highest-value route is paid work on the strength of having built this. Cheapest test: the drafted teacher emails with a price and a pay link.
- **Verdict:** FIX FIRST (judged by Claude in the session of 17 November; the owner then chose "sell before building").
- **Biggest risk:** that the selling does not happen. Building is cheap and has been the hiding place; the plan fails if the emails and posts go unsent like the 27 actions before them.
- **10-minute test:** send your own Daily result and its challenge link to ten people you know today, and ask each what confused them.
- **Flip to BUILD:** by 16 January 2027, 3 paid class pre-orders from 60 priced emails; or by 20 December 2026, 10% or more of the people who finish a Daily coming back on another day or leaving an email, after at least 500 real visitors from the sends. If neither by 31 January, it stops being an income project and stays up as a portfolio piece.

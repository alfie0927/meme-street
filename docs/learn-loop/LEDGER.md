## Final report (after 12 iterations, 2026-11-08)

- **B, the automated checks:** 9 of 9 pass (from 6 of 9 at the start). The six core units are interleaved (97% of questions under their section), no lesson is thin (72 lessons have 3 exercises, 19 have 4, 4 have 5), the right answer is the longest option in 36% of core-unit questions (from 71%), no near-duplicates, every chart lesson has a chart or a test, every link resolves, every task kind is tested.
- **C, the personas:** beginner 17/20 (from 12), week two 14/20 (from 13), trader 12/20 (first run). Target was 18 each: not reached. Twelve iterations fixed 31 findings and committed each one locally; nothing was deployed.
- **Needs the owner (design decisions, not taken by the loop):**
  - Wrong answers earn 2 XP and still count towards a pass, so a lesson can be passed by clicking. Options: wrong answers earn 0 and a pass needs two-thirds right (the misses come round again), or keep "every exercise done" as the pass and accept that XP measures effort, not knowledge.
  - The crowded first screen for a player with 0 XP (M), and the one-line meaning plus lesson link per trading-card number (M).

## 2026-10-03 - Meme Street real-money pivot
- **Idea:** Trading game with invented companies (150+, 30 industries), live LLM news wire, long/short, limit/stop orders, 2x/3x leveraged products. House is counterparty and takes 0.1% fee per side; daily contest drives retention. Play-money now, real-money business is the goal.
- **Verdict:** FIX FIRST
- **Biggest risk:** Players cannot verify the operator isn't rigging news and prices, so nobody deposits real money, and play-money testing can't reveal that. Legal (gambling/derivatives licensing) is the second gate.
- **10-minute test:** Post a $5 refundable "founding member" paid-contest link (Stripe payment link) to the existing testers and see who clicks and pays; no code needed.
- **Flip to BUILD:** Run the paid-entry test with a daily signed hash of the news/price seed published (commit-reveal) and get a quick legal opinion on the cheapest structure (skill contest, closed currency). BUILD if >=5% of testers with 3+ active days pay $5-20 within 7 days and ~40% repeat, and legal cost is under ~$150k / 6 months. Below 1-2% it is a free toy; do not build real-money rails.

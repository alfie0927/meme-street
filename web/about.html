How it works

News moves prices
Rumours, earnings, launches, lawsuits and the odd shock, about companies with their own bosses, products and rivals. Read the wire, then decide.
Trade anything
Stocks, the MSI 50 index of the 50 biggest companies, sector funds, 2x and 3x products and tiny "moonshots". Go long or short, leave limit, stop-loss and take-profit orders, and trade calls and puts that move with the stock.
Fair by design
Nobody's trade moves a price, ours included. Prices follow the news, the economy and how traders act around trends and price levels, so skill shows, while simple tricks like buying every dip don't beat holding the market. We check that by simulating many markets with dozens of strategies.
Learn as you go
Short lessons on charts, risk and the economy, each a few minutes long, to read whenever you like.
Compete
A new leaderboard every day, a daily contest where everyone starts with the same paper money, and the Daily Chart: two minutes on the same mystery chart as everyone else, a new one each day, with streaks and a result to share. Ranks count your returns, so buying MB can't buy a better rank.
Real costs
Every trade costs 0.1% of its size, like a broker's fee, so you learn to count costs. There's no hidden spread.

Questions

Is it real money?

No. Memebucks are practice money for learning to trade. They have no cash value and are never paid out, so you can make mistakes without risking your own money.

Are these real companies?

No. Every company, person, product and headline is made up. The numbers behave like a market, but nothing here is investment advice.

Can I lose everything?

You can lose your balance, especially by shorting or trading moonshots. A short ties up its amount from your cash, and one that loses too much is closed for you.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The pages fit a phone screen, and you can watch the live market without signing in.

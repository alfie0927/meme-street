Trading journal

Write down every trade: the setup, the plan (where the stop and the target are) and, afterwards, what you learned. An entry made before you trade becomes an open trade when you open a position in that stock, and is closed with its result when you close it. After a few dozen trades it shows you which setups work for you.

Economic calendar

The economy behind the market reports on a schedule: weekly jobless claims, monthly consumer prices (CPI), jobs, retail sales and a business survey, quarterly growth (GDP), and eight interest-rate decisions a game year. Each release has a forecast published a few game days ahead. Prices react to the surprise, the gap between the figure and the forecast, not to the figure itself. Hot inflation and strong jobs can mean higher interest rates, which weigh on growth companies and property and help banks. Volatility is usually low just before a release and high just after it.

The calendar runs on game days: one game day is 30 real minutes, so a game week passes in three and a half hours and a game month in about fifteen. Each day shows the real time it starts. Click a day for its details.
